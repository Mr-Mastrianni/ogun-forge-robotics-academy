Technology grown from nature's blueprint

Kamitron is an independent research and engineering studio. We work where robotics, living systems, ancestral knowledge and digital worlds meet.

Nature is our first reference. Termite mounds, fungal networks and the human body solve problems engineers are still learning to solve. We study them, build from them and teach what we learn.

We hold ourselves to one standard: say clearly what is established science, what is a working prototype, what is traditional knowledge and what is imagination. Wonder without confusion.

Founded by Corey Mastrianni

Corey is a multidisciplinary technologist working across AI, software engineering, robotics and nature-inspired technology. He also leads CAAKE, Kamitron's sister company, which builds healthcare and enterprise technology. CAAKE solves the problems institutions have today. Kamitron explores what becomes possible next.
